# Explanations

* [Mappings and the SSSOM set](mappings.md) - Where the schema crosses to another vocabulary: the five rows DPV publishes for this edition, each held to the pinned copy of DPV's file. The SSSOM set and the other mappings of the old schema are gone.
* [Modelling choices](modelling-choices.md) - Why the schema has these shapes: records that refer by identifier, an actor's kind told apart by a designator, principles held by subclause number, and fields that go beyond the framework named as an adopter's.
* [Scope](scope.md) - What the model leaves out: the text of the standard, a clause number for a term or a lower subclause, a fixed method of risk assessment, and the rules no validator checks.
