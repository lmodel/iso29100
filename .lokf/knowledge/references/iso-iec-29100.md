# ISO/IEC 29100:2024

ISO's record 85938 gives the second edition, published on 2024-02-16. It replaces records 45123 and 73722, ISO/IEC 29100:2011 and ISO/IEC 29100:2011/Amd 1:2018.[^extract]

The schema header carries `version: "2024"` and ISO's page for the edition as `source`.[^schema] The standard is copyright ISO and IEC, and this repository and bundle hold none of its text. NOTICE gives the position.[^notice]
